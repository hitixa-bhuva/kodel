Our Global Journey

Our story is one of growth, moving from world-class manufacturing to a premium design experience.

2016 | The Foundation: Kodel Headquarters (India)

Our journey began at the Kodel manufacturing headquarters in India. For nearly 10 years, we have led the way in high-tech, animal-free leather production. By combining traditional craftsmanship with modern technology, our India HQ has become a global hub for durable, sustainable, and premium synthetic surfaces.

2025 | The Experience: Thailand Flagship Showroom

In 2025, we expanded our vision to Thailand by opening a premier 10,000+ sq. ft. flagship showroom. This luxury gallery is a dedicated space for architects, designers, and automotive experts to touch, feel, and explore the future of leather. It is where our manufacturing expertise meets world-class local service.
