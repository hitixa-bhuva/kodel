Care Guide

Leather Care Instruction

01

Clean

Wipe with a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and lukewarm water.

02

Rinse

Follow up with a clean, damp cloth to remove soap residue.

03

Dry

Always air dry; avoid direct heat or sunlight.

04

Test

Always spot-check a small, hidden area first.

Critical Maintenance

Avoid: Stay away from alcohol, harsh chemicals, or abrasive cleaners.

Prohibition: Do not soak or saturate the material.
